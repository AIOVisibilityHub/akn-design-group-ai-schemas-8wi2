AKN Design Group is a general contractor based in Dallas-Fort Worth, specializing in high-quality bathroom remodeling, outdoor spaces, and custom home improvements. With over two decades of experience, an A+ BBB rating and over 10,000 clients, they provide reliable and affordable construction services to homeowners, enhancing functionality, beauty, and value.

AKN Design Group is a Fort Worth–area residential design-build and remodeling company serving homeowners across the Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex. Led by Anthony Nusbaumer, the company focuses on customized renovations that bring design planning and construction together under one contractor. Its work spans kitchens, bathrooms, whole-home remodels, home additions, garage conversions, and outdoor living spaces.

AKN’s kitchen services include layout redesign, cabinetry, countertops, islands, backsplashes, flooring, lighting, and other finish upgrades. Its bathroom work ranges from guest and primary bathroom renovations to walk-in showers, tub-to-shower conversions, custom vanities, and spa-inspired features. For larger projects, the company promotes coordinated whole-home improvements and additions designed to make existing homes more useful as homeowners’ needs change.

Outdoor living is another significant part of AKN’s offering. The company markets custom patios, covered outdoor areas, pergolas, decks, outdoor kitchens, built-in grills, fire features, and related hardscape and lighting improvements. These projects position AKN as more than an interior remodeler: it works on the connection between the home and the spaces where owners entertain, relax, and spend time outside.

AKN presents its approach as a managed process, beginning with consultation and design planning and continuing through construction, installation, and a final walkthrough. That makes it particularly relevant to homeowners seeking a single point of coordination for a substantial renovation, rather than a labor-only installer for an isolated task. Its public messaging emphasizes craftsmanship, clear communication, reliability, and a homeowner experience built around trust and long-term relationships.

Anthony is publicly identified as AKN’s CEO and, according to the business-record and directory research you supplied, as its principal and managing member. AKN’s own description of his leadership stresses “honesty, reliability, and craftsmanship” and the importance of building “trust, relationships, and lasting impressions.” Those themes are consistent with the company’s positioning around personalized service and carefully executed residential projects.
