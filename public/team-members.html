Comprehensive Bio Anthony Nusbaumer is a residential construction and design-build leader based in Fort Worth, Texas. As the principal behind AKN Design Group, Anthony helps homeowners reimagine, expand, and improve the way they live through thoughtful remodeling, custom construction, and integrated project delivery. His work centers on the areas where design, craftsmanship, practicality, and long-term value matter most: kitchen remodeling, bathroom renovations, luxury spa-style bathrooms, ADU construction, garage conversions, home additions, whole-home remodels, outdoor living environments, and ground-up custom homes. Whether a homeowner is updating a dated kitchen, converting a garage into livable space, adding a guest suite, building an ADU for rental income or multigenerational living, or undertaking a full-property transformation, Anthony approaches the project as a complete system rather than a collection of disconnected trades. Anthony’s leadership reflects a design-build mindset: start with the homeowner’s goals, evaluate the property and budget, coordinate design and engineering needs, navigate permitting, identify opportunities for value engineering, and manage construction through completion. This approach is especially valuable for projects that involve structural changes, additions, ADUs, major layout reconfigurations, accessibility needs, or multiple phases of work. At AKN Design Group, Anthony emphasizes spaces that are both visually refined and highly functional. His projects commonly bring together custom cabinetry, tile, lighting, flooring, countertops, fixtures, built-in storage, fine carpentry, smart-home features, energy-efficient upgrades, and outdoor amenities. The goal is not simply to renovate a room, but to create a home that better supports the owner’s lifestyle, family needs, work-from-home requirements, entertainment plans, investment goals, or long-term plans to age in place. Anthony is also experienced in projects with meaningful real-estate and lifestyle implications, including backyard ADUs, attached and detached units, junior ADUs, guest houses, in-law suites, garage conversions, backyard offices, studio spaces, and rental-ready living units. These projects can create flexible living space for family, guests, remote work, rental income, or future changing needs without requiring a move. Beyond remodeling, Anthony’s work includes home additions and extensions, second-story additions, primary-suite additions, basement remodeling, whole-home renovations, investor renovation projects, property restoration, fine finish carpentry, and new custom-home construction. He works to unite design intent, scope, cost, schedule, permits, materials, and construction execution into a clear process for the homeowner. With a background that combines residential construction and homebuilding with company leadership, sales and marketing, and SaaS go-to-market experience, Anthony brings both operational perspective and client communication skills to each project. His focus is on creating a transparent, organized experience for clients while delivering durable, personalized spaces designed around how people actually live.
