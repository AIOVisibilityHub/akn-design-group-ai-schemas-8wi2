Kitchen Remodel: Dated Layout to Functional Entertaining Space

Service: Kitchen Remodeling

The client faced a significant challenge with their existing kitchen, which felt closed off and lacked essential functionality. Key issues included insufficient usable prep space, inadequate storage solutions, and a layout that failed to support family meals or entertaining guests. This created a restrictive environment that hindered daily life and social gatherings.

AKN Design Group addressed these challenges by undertaking a comprehensive redesign of the kitchen's layout. The primary goal was to significantly improve both circulation within the space and overall functionality. Following the layout redesign, AKN meticulously coordinated all elements of the renovation. This involved selecting and integrating custom cabinetry, countertops, backsplash tile, new flooring, and updated lighting. They also carefully planned the placement of new appliances and finish selections to create a cohesive and harmonious kitchen environment. This integrated approach ensured that every aspect of the remodel contributed to a unified vision.

This method of combining layout redesign with coordinated material and appliance integration was crucial for transforming a dated, closed-off kitchen into a modern, highly functional space. It highlights the importance of a holistic approach to kitchen remodeling, where every component works together to resolve existing issues and enhance the overall living experience, making the space ideal for both everyday use and entertaining.

Outcome: The result was a brighter, more efficient kitchen that offered increased storage capacity, significantly improved prep space, and a thoughtfully designed layout perfectly suited for both daily family use and hosting gatherings.

Bathroom Remodel: Everyday Bath to Spa-Style Retreat

Service: Bathroom Remodeling

The client's primary bathroom was outdated, presenting several challenges that detracted from comfort and functionality. These potential issues included an undersized shower, dated finishes that no longer matched their aesthetic preferences, inadequate lighting, and limited storage. Furthermore, the existing layout did not align with the homeowner's daily routines, making the space less practical and enjoyable.

AKN Design Group approached this project by completely reworking the bathroom's design, prioritizing comfort, enhanced function, and updated finishes. Their solution potentially involved a range of specific improvements tailored to the homeowners' needs. This could include installing a larger walk-in shower with a frameless glass enclosure, custom vanity solutions, upgraded tile for both the shower surround and general surfaces, and improved lighting schemes. Crucially, storage was customized to integrate seamlessly into the space while meeting the homeowners' specific requirements, potentially incorporating features like a freestanding tub, heated floors, and rainfall shower fixtures.

This comprehensive remodeling strategy, focusing on personalized comfort, functional improvements, and aesthetic upgrades, was key to transforming an ordinary bathroom into a spa-like retreat. It underscores how a detailed approach, addressing specific pain points and incorporating desired features, can significantly elevate a primary bathroom from a mere functional space to a luxurious and calming sanctuary that supports daily routines and provides a relaxing experience.

Outcome: The outcome was a more functional and aesthetically elevated primary bathroom. It successfully supported the homeowners' daily routines while simultaneously creating a calming, spa-inspired experience within their home.

Outdoor Living: Backyard Entertaining Upgrade

Service: Outdoor Space Renovation

The client possessed an underutilized backyard space that suffered from a lack of essential features for outdoor enjoyment. Specific challenges included minimal shade, insufficient seating options, and the complete absence of a dedicated area for outdoor cooking or entertaining guests. This meant the backyard wasn't serving its potential as an extension of the home's living space.

AKN Design Group's solution involved designing and constructing a complete outdoor living area. The entire space was meticulously organized around three core functions: providing ample shade, creating comfortable gathering spots, and establishing a fully functional outdoor cooking zone. The project highlighted AKN's ability to coordinate various complex elements, including the careful integration of hardscaping, the construction of a covered structure to provide shade, strategic placement of lighting, and the incorporation of various outdoor amenities such as an outdoor kitchen, built-in grill, fire pit or fireplace, and storage solutions.

This integrated approach was vital in transforming a neglected backyard into a vibrant and functional outdoor destination. It demonstrates how thoughtful design and coordinated construction, encompassing both aesthetic and practical elements like shade, cooking facilities, and gathering areas, can significantly expand a home's usable living space and make a backyard far more practical and inviting for everyday relaxation and entertaining.

Outcome: The project resulted in a well-defined outdoor destination that successfully expanded the home's usable living space. The backyard became significantly more practical and inviting for both everyday relaxation and entertaining guests.

Home Addition: More Space Without Moving

Service: Custom Home Improvements

The client faced the challenge of needing more usable space within their home but desired to avoid the disruption and cost associated with moving to a new residence. Their requirements could have included a new primary suite, a dedicated home office, an additional guest room, an expanded family room, or a space for multigenerational living. The critical constraint was the desire to remain in their valued neighborhood, which made a home addition the ideal solution over relocating.

AKN Design Group managed the entire design-build process for the home addition. This comprehensive approach started with initial scope development and meticulous layout planning. AKN then took responsibility for coordinating all aspects of permitting, ensuring compliance with local regulations. Following this, they oversaw the construction phase, including the critical structural integration of the new addition with the existing home. Finally, AKN managed the selection and installation of interior finishes and ensured a seamless exterior tie-in, making the addition appear as an original part of the house.

This design-build model, where AKN served as the single point of contact from concept to completion, was crucial for homeowners seeking to expand their living space without the complexities of coordinating multiple contractors. It underscores the value of an integrated process that handles everything from permits to finishes, ensuring the addition not only meets current needs but also blends aesthetically and structurally with the original home, allowing clients to gain space while staying in their beloved neighborhood.

Outcome: The outcome was a seamless home addition that successfully added valuable square footage to the property. This new space effectively supported the household's current needs and was visually integrated to blend perfectly with the original home's aesthetic.

Whole-Home Remodel: Coordinated Transformation

Service: Whole-Home Remodel

The client's house suffered from a lack of cohesion and functionality, presenting multiple challenges. These included inconsistent finishes throughout the home, inefficient room flow that hindered daily living, and dated kitchens and bathrooms that no longer met modern standards or the homeowner's lifestyle. The overall layout of the house was not serving the household's current needs, leading to a fragmented and uninspiring living environment.

AKN Design Group's solution involved creating and executing a single, coordinated renovation plan that encompassed the entire home. This comprehensive approach began with strategic sequencing of demolition and any necessary structural changes to optimize the layout and flow. Following this, AKN undertook complete renovations of both the kitchen and bathrooms. The plan also included updating flooring throughout the home, applying fresh paint, making necessary drywall repairs, upgrading lighting fixtures, installing custom built-ins, and completing finish carpentry. Every element, from interior doors to trim work, was carefully considered to ensure a unified aesthetic.

This integrated approach was critical for transforming a disjointed and outdated house into a cohesive and functional home. It highlights the importance of a single, overarching plan for whole-home renovations, allowing for consistent style, improved efficiency, and a unified aesthetic across all rooms. By managing the entire project as one, AKN ensured that all improvements worked in harmony, rather than appearing as piecemeal updates, resulting in a truly transformative outcome that catered to the household's lifestyle.

Outcome: The result was a cohesive home where all rooms felt connected in both style and function. All improvements were planned and executed as one unified project, ensuring a harmonious transformation rather than disparate upgrades.

Design-Build: Complex Renovation With One Accountable Team

Service: Custom Home Improvements

The client desired to renovate several areas within their home, but they lacked a clear and organized process to manage the complexity of such a project. Key challenges included difficulties with decision-making, budgeting, scope coordination between different trades, and effectively sequencing the construction work. They needed a streamlined approach to navigate the intricacies of a multi-area renovation.

AKN Design Group stepped in as the central project partner, offering a comprehensive design-build solution. They guided the client through every stage, starting with initial planning and assisting with material and finish selections. AKN then provided accurate estimating and facilitated design coordination to ensure all elements aligned with the client's vision. Where necessary, they provided support for permit acquisition and developed detailed construction scheduling. Crucially, AKN managed all trade coordination, ensuring smooth workflow between different specialists, conducted thorough quality-control walkthroughs, and oversaw the completion of the final punch-list items.

This design-build model was vital for homeowners undertaking complex renovations, providing a single point of accountability and streamlining a potentially overwhelming process. It emphasizes the significant advantage of having one dedicated team manage the project from the initial concept through to final construction. This approach ensures greater organization, clear communication, and a cohesive outcome, alleviating the client's burden of coordinating multiple entities and offering a more predictable and less stressful remodeling experience.

Outcome: The project resulted in a more organized and streamlined remodeling experience for the client. With AKN Design Group serving as one accountable team, the entire project was coordinated effectively from the early concept phase through to completed construction.
